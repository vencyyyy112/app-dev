# My Favorite Series & Movies: One Piece

## Overview
**One Piece** is my all-time favorite franchise because *it combines incredible world-building, emotional storytelling, and unforgettable adventures across the Grand Line*. Whether watching the anime series or theatrical films like **One Piece Film: Red**, every arc delivers memorable moments.

---

### Quick Facts
* **Creator:** Eiichiro Oda
* **Genre:** Action, Adventure, Fantasy
* **Main Protagonist:** Monkey D. Luffy
* **Rating:** 10/10

---

### Top 3 Favorite Straw Hat Pirates
1. **Monkey D. Luffy** – Captain of the Straw Hat Pirates and user of the Gum-Gum Fruit.
2. **Roronoa Zoro** – The crew's swordsman who wields the Three-Sword Style.
3. **Sanji** – The crew's cook and martial artist known as "Black Leg."
